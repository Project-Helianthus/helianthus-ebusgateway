## Admit the northbound output hook at registration

A northbound or both driver descriptor may now declare the single hook `output` at hook version `1`, and the registry keeps that declaration in the frozen entry. An empty hook list still registers on every direction. `output` on a southbound descriptor fails with `ErrHookDirection`, a missing or other version fails with `ErrUnsupportedHookVersion`, and a repeated declaration fails with `ErrDuplicateHook`. The reserved names still fail as unimplemented and every other name as unknown. A refused descriptor installs nothing. Start, stop and health stay with the driver manager: health is its snapshot's observed state, reason and safety-quarantine flag. Registering the hook publishes nothing, and the southbound eeBUS descriptor is unchanged (#1125). The design is in `docs/architecture/northbound-output-hook.md`.
