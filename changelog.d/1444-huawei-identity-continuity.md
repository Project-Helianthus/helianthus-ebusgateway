## Report a Huawei child's identity-link state and binding headroom in the instance status

Each child in the Huawei SmartLogger and S-Dongle instance status now reports the state of its identity link in the publishing generation (`identity_link_state`: qualified, candidate or withdrawn) and the number of native bindings and source descriptors its PV asset holds (`binding_incarnations` and `source_descriptors`), counted against the bounds of 128 and 32 that the tool schema states. The members are JSON fields of the existing status, carry no metric label, are read without I/O and are absent while the asset or its link does not exist. The growth measurement found that kernel compaction of withdrawn incarnations is needed, so the publication itself is unchanged: a child that loses its identity still has its link withdrawn and receives a new binding incarnation on re-admission (#1444). The gateway binary and the MCP status output change.
