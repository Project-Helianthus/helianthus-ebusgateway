## Measure binding, identity-link and source-descriptor growth across Huawei identity-loss cycles

An offline test measures, on both Huawei replay fixtures, how the source descriptors, bindings, identity links, fences and cursors of a PV asset grow per identity-loss cause, states the events and the time to each kernel bound, and probes whether the pinned kernel accepts a qualified-candidate-qualified identity-link round trip. Every loss followed by re-admission adds one binding and one identity link, so the binding bound is reached after 127 such events; a transient failure that the native provider reports stale adds nothing, and every operator restart adds one source descriptor, one cursor, one binding and one identity link, so the 32nd restart is refused. The kernel accepts the round trip. The composition page records the table and the selected remedy: identity continuity in the gateway driver, absence hysteresis over three complete listings, and kernel compaction of withdrawn incarnations (#1444). Production behaviour and the gateway binary are unchanged.
