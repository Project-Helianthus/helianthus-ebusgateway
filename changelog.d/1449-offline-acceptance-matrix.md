## Add the offline acceptance matrix with its graph axis and its validator

The repository carries the offline acceptance matrix of the release: device families by surfaces, the graph axis, the conditions and the graph negatives, in `docs/testing/offline-acceptance-matrix.json` with its page `docs/testing/offline-acceptance-matrix.md`. A validator, `scripts/offline_acceptance_matrix.py`, ties every passing cell to an existing test, requires a reason for every excluded cell and keeps the page's tables equal to the matrix; the local gate runs it with its tests. Every cell starts unfilled: no cell passes yet, and only the semantic publication cells of the Huawei energy manager family, which its native contract withholds, are recorded as outside the release with their owner issue. Documentation and tooling only; the gateway binary is unchanged.
