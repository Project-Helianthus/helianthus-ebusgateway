## Publish boiler status, system, energy and regulator capability from native views

Boiler status, system, energy and the regulator capability are published by the `vaillantSemanticPublication` owner from native views: the acquisition side hands over a detached boiler snapshot, a system view that carries the resolved hot water bivalence bound, and the native controller capability, and the owner maps them to GraphQL values. Energy acquisition reaches the energy ledger through a native interface of reference times, channel, usage and window strings and register values; the owner derives the provider's keys. The thermal control callback clears the native flow temperature and asks the owner to withdraw the published one. The boiler B509 refresh is split per tier without reordering reads. Published output is unchanged; the gateway binary changes.
