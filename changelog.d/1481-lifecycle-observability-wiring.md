## Move the observability and source-selection artifact wiring out of runGatewayLifecycle

The startup source-selection setup and the bus-observability providers move from `runGatewayLifecycle` into named functions in `cmd/gateway/gateway_lifecycle_observability.go`, called at the same points in the same order. The startup source-selection setup covers the admission-path resolution with its log lines, the explicit-source override, the startup source-selection metrics, the artifact mode and explicit source, the delayed artifact emission and the final emission at shutdown. The bus-observability providers cover the semantic metrics provider, the adapter multiplexer diagnostic, arbitration-hold and escape-pair providers, the v8 rollout counters with their `/debug/vars` mirror, the v8 admin-events classifier provider, the baseline evidence counts of the source-selection artifact, and the startup surface, energy passive-state and energy freshness providers. Log lines, metric and expvar names, the artifact path and the shutdown order are unchanged. The Modbus RTU gate list and the EVSE boundary check include the new file. The gateway binary changes.
