## Route grant and START decisions through the transition function and split tryGrantAndStartForBids

The AdapterMux handles a grant attempt in named steps that take their decisions from the transition function: the admission gates (a START in flight, the absorb barrier, an outstanding blocking call, the regrant fence and the arbitration hold) decided over the typed state at the SYN; the selection, where the existing arbitration decision also refuses a winner while the bus has an owner; the loser notification; the START deadline arming, whose expiry asks the transition function whether a START is in flight; and the START write, either the non-blocking request or the blocking call in its own goroutine under the shutdown guard. The outcome of the START write comes back under the same lock as a second decision over the re-derived typed state, where the blocking call's return or failure is the transition function's START call event. With this slice the four transition handlers are under the size budget and table-tested. Wire behaviour, timing and effect order are unchanged; the gateway binary changes.
