## A control gate for every contributor of a shared hub asset

Every hub asset now keeps one control gate per publishing source, its owner and each declared contributor. A fence, retirement or transition cover of one source closes only that source's gate, so an unfinished, unsettled or abandoned control of one contributor no longer refuses another contributor's publications or delays its activation, and a transition cover fences only the transitioning source's generation. Reads of the asset stay withheld while any contributor's gate is open. Every control event reaches the owner's and each contributor's reprojection callback in `HubSeq` order. A coherent or exact capture admits the asset when at least one contributor is admitted with a published, unfenced cursor of its current generation, and the captured snapshot keeps every contributor's source descriptor and fenced cursors. A production hub now accepts a contributor list; no production declaration names a contributor yet (#1539). For assets with one source the publication, read, capture and reprojection results are unchanged; no published output, golden, identifier or wire value changes. The gateway binary changes, so affected release evidence must be renewed before the final bill of materials freeze.
