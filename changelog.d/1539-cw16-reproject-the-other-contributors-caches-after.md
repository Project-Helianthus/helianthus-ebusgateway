## Reprojection of the other contributors' caches after a publication on a shared hub asset

When one contributor of a shared hub asset commits a publication, the hub now queues a reprojection of every other publishing source's family cache, the owner's and each other contributor's, on its existing reprojection scheduler in `HubSeq` order. Each of those callbacks receives the asset's current snapshot once, as a `ControlEvent` of kind `published` without a control identifier, outside every hub lock; a publication that commits while the callbacks run is delivered again before the receivers settle. Reads, `Settled` and coherent or exact captures of the asset stay withheld until every receiver has acknowledged the latest publication it is owed, and `Resettle` retries a receiver whose callback failed; no publication waits for a peer reprojection. A control reprojection that delivers a newer view settles the peer reprojections it covers. No production declaration names a contributor yet (#1539). An asset with one source queues nothing: its publication, read, capture and reprojection results, the event stream and every golden, identifier and wire value are unchanged. The gateway binary changes, so affected release evidence must be renewed before the final bill of materials freeze.
