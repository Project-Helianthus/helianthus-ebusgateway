## Portal resources and Matter projections for assets with several sources

The Portal catalog keeps a resource whose asset holds several sources. Per source it applies the single-source rules (one service instance, one capability instance on it, a current binding and source epoch), keeps the owner's path, identified by the publication's declared source and not by its driver, when the owner carries the capability and otherwise the path of the one contributor whose binding carries it, and attributes the resource and its actions to the driver of that binding under that driver's own admitted descriptor. A resource that is not kept now carries a reason in the catalog source's capture diagnostics instead of disappearing silently; without a source-to-driver resolution or the owner's source an asset with several current sources is reported as `contributor_driver_unresolved`. The gateway composition does not supply that resolution yet, so production output follows the single-source rule. The Matter projection decides eligibility on the keys its mapping reads, so a conflict or a stale fact on another key of the asset no longer refuses the whole snapshot, and the binding feed builds an operation claim from the action's own binding and refuses it when its resource names another path.

Single-source output is unchanged: the Portal catalog, Portal source capture and Matter feed characterization goldens and the Matter contract hash constants do not move.
