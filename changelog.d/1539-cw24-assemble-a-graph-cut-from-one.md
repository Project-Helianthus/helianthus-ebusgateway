## Assemble a graph cut from one coherent hub capture and report what the cut does not include

`Hub.CaptureCut` reads every declared semantic hub asset in one capture under the hub read lock, with the same admission and exposure rules as the per-asset capture, and assembles one graph cut from the admitted snapshots with SemReg's `AssembleCut` and the element catalog compiled into the process. A relation whose far end is held by another asset is now judged across assets: it is `coherent` when that asset is a member and holds the far end current. The hub asserts completeness only when every declared asset was included; it returns the cut, its canonical bytes and every declared asset the cut does not include, each with one reason: `source_unavailable`, `publication_withheld` or `excluded` (a duplicate identity finding). An asset declared without graph enforcement that publishes facts of a catalog-covered pack is a member and is reported as `ungraphed`. A commit that becomes ready during the capture waits for it, so a cut never holds two snapshots of one asset. There is no global revision: a cut's identity is the vector of its member snapshot identifiers. The per-asset capture results and reasons are unchanged, and no read surface uses cuts yet (#1539).
