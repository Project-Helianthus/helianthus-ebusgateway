## Publish operator records from the configuration provider as a contributor source of their home asset

The gateway's configuration provider is now a hub-native publisher of operator records (`cmd/gateway/operator_record_publisher.go`). A configured record whose starting end is a driver's resource, such as a relation instance from an inverter, is published on the driver's asset by the provider as a declared contributor, on its own source, epoch and generation. It is published while the driver's generation is fenced, survives the fence, and can be withdrawn while the driver is stopped. A resource that only configuration knows is homed in an asset whose single source is the provider, under an operator slot anchor. Every record carries an operator origin with the audit evidence of its declaration and its own revision; batches are built under the asset commit lock from the current snapshot. A record set whose relation starting end does not resolve, that lacks the declaration digest, or that repeats a key is refused with a reason before anything is submitted. The preflight wires the provider with an empty record set, so no source is admitted and no output changes. On a shared asset the hub does not yet refuse a driver's revision or withdrawal of the provider's graph records; `docs/architecture/operator-records.md` describes the provider, its refusals, its lifecycle against fences and that limit (#1539).
