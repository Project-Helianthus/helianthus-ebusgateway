## Prove the infrastructure pack graph-enforced offline with a configured meter role

A new test (`cmd/gateway/semantic_infrastructure_graph_test.go`) runs the infrastructure pack graph-enforced on synthetic input. A synthetic site publisher declared with enforcement on observes a site, its grid connection and one meter; it publishes import and export active power keyed by the grid connection and import and export energy keyed by the meter. An operator-record section declares the meter role as an `infrastructure.relation.grid_connection_meter` instance and the site's `infrastructure.relation.site_grid_connection` instance; the configuration provider publishes both into the grid connection's home asset with basis class `configuration`, and both are `coherent` edges of a complete cut. The role leaves every fact unchanged, and power keyed by the meter or energy keyed by the grid connection is still refused (`invalid_value` or `identity_drift`). An intent on the grid connection is refused without a send (`definition_owner_missing`) both through a capability the publisher does not publish and through the meter's read capability. The new page `docs/architecture/semantic-family-graph-state.md` lists every composed family with its graph state: EVSE, PV, storage and thermal are not yet in the graph, and infrastructure is proven offline without a production publisher. No production code changes (#1539).
