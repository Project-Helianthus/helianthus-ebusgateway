## Pin the Matter 1.6.1 roster derived from the element catalog

`matter/model161.go` now pins the SHA-256 of the adopted Matter 1.6.1 roster (`DocsSemanticContractSHA256161`, beside `DocsSemanticContractPath161`) to the docs-semantic roster derived from the element catalog, which adds service and relation rows. A new test reads the roster from an immutable docs-semantic checkout named by `HELIANTHUS_DOCS_SEMANTIC_ROOT` (or the repository-local staged reference), refuses bytes under another digest or path, and checks that its fact, capability and operation rows equal the shared ledger while service and relation rows are left to the endpoint work of the Matter binding; any other row kind is refused. `docs/architecture/matter-output-binding-v1.md` names the new pin. The projection, its output, the 1.7 contract constants and the binding feed are unchanged (#1539).
