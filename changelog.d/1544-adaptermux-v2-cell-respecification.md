## Write the procedures of AD04, AD07 and AD09 and reserve the FIFO-order cells

The adapter-action cells AD04 (adapter reset) and AD09 (host-link disconnect and reconnect) and the arbitration cell AD07 (address-priority selection between the gateway session and an external ebusd session) of the `adaptermux-v2` matrix have written procedures, pass criteria and read-only smoke checks with offline tests; a check whose AdapterMux series the gateway does not publish ends with its own exit status and names the series. The four FIFO-order cells PX05–PX08 of the `ENS` and `ENH` rows are reserved under the new category PL-04 (`planned`, `fifo_order_superseded_by_address_priority`) because address priority replaced FIFO order. No cell awaits re-specification any more, and the matrix inventory version is 2. Tooling and test inventory only; the gateway binary is unchanged.
