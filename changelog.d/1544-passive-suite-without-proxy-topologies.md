## Carry the proxy-dependent passive cases by matrix cells and start the remaining passive cases the way the add-on does

The passive suite has three cases (`P01`, `P02`, `P06`). The three proxy-dependent cases are carried by `adaptermux-v2` cells named on the policy page, which also names the part of them no cell carries; the cells' checks and the passive gate's inputs are unchanged. The passive harness starts the gateway the way the add-on does and fails when the eBUS driver is withheld. Gate and tooling change, the gateway binary changes only through the matrix package.
