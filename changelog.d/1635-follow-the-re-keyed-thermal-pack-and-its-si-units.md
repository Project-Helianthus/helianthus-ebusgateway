## Follow the re-keyed thermal pack and its SI units in the thermal mappers and metric rows

The gateway now pins the SemReg revision whose thermal pack keys every field by one resource dimension and declares SI units; the three SemReg mapping gate scripts name the same revision. Each thermal mapper names the field and resource dimension of the accepted placement: the boiler flow is `thermal.measurement.heat_generator_flow_temperature` under `thermal.dimension.heat_generator`, the circuit flow candidate names `thermal.measurement.circuit_flow_temperature` under `thermal.dimension.circuit`, the measured hot-water candidate names `thermal.measurement.dhw_temperature` under `thermal.dimension.dhw`, room temperature, humidity and the zone setpoint name the zone dimension and the hot-water setpoint the hot-water dimension. The dimension value stays the native identity text the mapper used before; resource identifiers and relation instances come with the graph publication of the thermal family, which stays outside the graph. Units are converted once at the source boundary and exactly: degree Celsius + 273.15 to kelvin and percent / 100 to a 0 to 1 ratio, while the native value and its unit (`native.celsius`) stay in the evidence. A humidity register read through a circuit no longer names the circuit service, because its subject is a zone. The boiler's native compatibility scalar stays in degree Celsius. The hot-water setpoint stays withheld until graph publication allocates the hot-water resource.

The thermal metric rows follow the pack metadata: one row per re-keyed field, the registry value exported unchanged in kelvin, a 0 to 1 ratio, cubic metre per second or watt, and a slot exports only through a service whose fact-key dimension is the field's dimension. `helianthus_thermal_dhw_setpoint_celsius` becomes `helianthus_thermal_dhw_setpoint_kelvin`; the replaced `helianthus_thermal_water_temperature_celsius` gives way to `helianthus_thermal_circuit_flow_temperature_kelvin`, `helianthus_thermal_dhw_temperature_kelvin` and `helianthus_thermal_heat_generator_flow_temperature_kelvin`; `helianthus_thermal_power_watts` now carries `thermal.measurement.thermal_power`; the `unit` label of the air temperature and temperature setpoint series names kelvin. No other series is renamed. The unit rule is decided (SI in the registry, customary units only at display in a consumer); its strict reading is a working reading not yet confirmed. The Matter feed golden and the item count of the Matter projection report, the metric inventory, its coverage page, the naming inventory and the measured hot-water page follow (#1635).
