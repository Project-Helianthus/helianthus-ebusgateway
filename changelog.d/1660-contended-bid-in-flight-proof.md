## State the limit of the contended-bid in-flight proof and make its stimulus tests deterministic

The PX01 and PX02 stimuli of the `adaptermux-v2` matrix runner keep taking the contender's bid as in flight once a received symbol follows a SYN that arrived after the INFO answer, so a bus that carries only SYNs still completes the proof. The runner now states the recorded limit of that proof: a START queued between the bid snapshot of a SYN and the delivery of that SYN, or a grant step that declines at an admission or selection gate, leaves the bid queued while the proof passes, until the mux exposes a hand-off signal the runner can read. The fake mux of the stimulus tests holds a fenced bid's result until the contender acts on it and handles a closed session before it serves the next one, so the PX01 and PX02 stimulus tests no longer depend on machine load. The header of `scripts/adaptermux_v2_smoke.py` now states what its check does: AD04 and AD09 measure resumption from the reading before the one that first shows the discontinuity, and a cell whose required series is absent ends with `signal_missing`. Tooling only; the gateway binary is unchanged.
