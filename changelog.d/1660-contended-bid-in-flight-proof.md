## Count a contended bid as in flight only after the grant step that saw it

The PX01 and PX02 stimuli of the `adaptermux-v2` matrix runner replace or abandon the contender's bid only after the grant step that saw it. The mux takes the bid snapshot of a SYN before it delivers that SYN, so a START queued in between can have its INFO answer reach the contender before that SYN while that SYN's grant step leaves the bid queued. The contender now counts a SYN only when another symbol arrived after the INFO answer and before the SYN, and concludes once one more symbol follows that SYN. The tests drive this interleaving. The fake mux holds a fenced bid's result until the contender acts on it, and it handles a closed session before it serves the next one, so the PX01 and PX02 stimulus tests no longer depend on timing. The header of `scripts/adaptermux_v2_smoke.py` now states what its check does: AD04 and AD09 measure resumption from the reading before the one that first shows the discontinuity, and a cell whose required series is absent ends with `signal_missing`. Tooling only; the gateway binary is unchanged.
