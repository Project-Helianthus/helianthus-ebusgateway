## Count the eighteen eeBUS heating leaves as declared from the specifications

The captured multi-leaf promotion registry marks its eighteen eeBUS heating leaves `SPECIFICATION_DECLARED_PUBLISHED`: support is declared from the cited specification and published, and a device capture is never a condition for it. The four retired candidates keep `WITHHELD` and their terminal states. The two-window captured comparison, its comparator classes and its numeric and native match rules are unchanged. Its outcome becomes the leaf's recorded verification level: `VERIFIED_BY_CAPTURE` when both windows match, `CAPTURE_MISMATCH` when a window compares and differs, and `NOT_RUN` when no comparison completes on admissible samples. A mismatch is a recorded finding next to the published leaf, never a withdrawal. The positive visibility is `PUBLISHED`, replacing `LOCKED_NOT_EXPOSED`, and the registry rejects a leaf with any other status. The promotion lock replaces its zero-promotion profile with `DECLARED_FROM_SPECIFICATION`, in which all eighteen leaves count as promoted and the consumer gate is open. A source fact that names a declared leaf must carry the same fact hash. The ordering, redaction and result-hash checks stay, and the lock contracts and fixtures are edited in place without a version change (#1694).
