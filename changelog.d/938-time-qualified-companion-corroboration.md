## Admit a passive companion only after two positive acknowledgements five seconds apart or a current identity witness

The address-table inserter admits the canonical companion of a passively observed source only after two positive acknowledgements at least five seconds apart (`PassiveCompanionCorroborationInterval`, zero or negative falls back to five seconds), or after one positive acknowledgement when the registry holds a current qualified identity witness for that exact source; the registry derives the companion, checks the witness and admits the passive target slot in one step. Before, the second acknowledgement admitted it regardless of elapsed time. The broadcast path uses the same interval for the broadcaster and its companion. Repeated, reordered or zero timestamps, negative acknowledgements, the gateway's own source and source bytes without a canonical companion never admit one, and the corroboration state holds at most one entry per canonical source. Source and target insertion on the first observation is unchanged (#938). This is passive-path work: the passive rows are re-run in the consolidated hardware session. The gateway binary changes.
